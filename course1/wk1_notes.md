## Glossary
*Supervised*: error correction provided by human-in-the-loop
*Regression*: Infinite, numeric outputs (120.5, -5, 0, etc.)
*Classification*: Discrete outputs (yes, no, fat, skinny, dog, bull, etc.)
 - Outputs _classes_ and output _categories_ are synonymous
